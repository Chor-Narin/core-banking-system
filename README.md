## 🚀 What is a Core Banking System?

A **Core Banking System (CBS)** is the central processing backbone of any financial institution. It enables real-time management of customer accounts, deposits, loans, transactions, and more — across branches, digital channels, and ATMs — from a single, unified platform.

Modern CBS platforms (2025–2026 era) are cloud-native, API-first, modular, and built for real-time processing, open banking, and instant scalability.

This repository documents / implements key features, architecture decisions, modules, and best practices of a contemporary core banking engine.

## ✨ Key Features (2026 Standards)

| #  | Feature                              | Description                                                                                          | Why It Matters (2026)                              |
|----|--------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| 1  | **Real-time Transaction Processing** | Instant posting of deposits, withdrawals, transfers, payments (incl. RTP, FedNow, SEPA Instant)     | Customers expect Amazon-like speed                  |
| 2  | **Centralized Account Management**   | Multi-currency savings, current, term deposits, wallets, virtual accounts, IBAN generation           | Single source of truth across all channels          |
| 3  | **Deposit & Liability Products**     | Flexible savings, fixed/recurring deposits, youth/senior accounts, interest calculation & accrual   | Core revenue engine for retail & corporate banking  |
| 4  | **Loan & Credit Management**         | Loan origination, servicing, EMI scheduling, interest & penalty calc, collateral tracking           | Fast credit decisioning + lower NPA risk            |
| 5  | **Modular & API-first Architecture** | Microservices / composable design + rich REST/GraphQL APIs for fintech & channel integration        | Enables Banking-as-a-Service & open banking         |
| 6  | **Cloud-native & Scalable Design**   | Kubernetes-ready, auto-scaling, multi-region HA, zero-downtime upgrades                             | 99.99% uptime + cost efficiency                     |
| 7  | **Omnichannel Integration**          | Seamless support for mobile, internet banking, USSD, agents, ATMs, branches                         | Unified 360° customer view                          |
| 8  | **KYC / AML / Compliance Engine**    | Built-in customer onboarding, CDD, PEP/sanctions screening, transaction monitoring, FATF alignment | Regulatory-grade automation                         |
| 9  | **Multi-entity & Multi-currency**    | Support for group banking, subsidiaries, cross-border operations, FX handling                      | Essential for regional & international banks        |
| 10 | **Real-time & Batch General Ledger** | Double-entry accounting, sub-ledger → GL posting, reconciliation, multi-GAAP reporting             | Accurate financials in real time                    |
| 11 | **Embedded Analytics & BI**          | Customer 360°, risk scoring, behavioral insights, churn prediction, built-in dashboards            | Data-driven product & risk decisions                |
| 12 | **Security & Fraud Prevention**      | Role-based access, encryption at rest/transit, behavioral biometrics, real-time fraud rules        | Zero-trust banking in high-threat environment       |
| 13 | **Payments Hub Integration**         | Cards (debit/credit/prepaid), ACH, wire, instant payments, open banking PSD2/OBIE APIs             | One platform for all payment rails                  |
| 14 | **Extensibility & Customization**    | Product factory, rule engines, low-code/no-code configuration for new products & workflows         | Launch new offerings in days, not months            |
